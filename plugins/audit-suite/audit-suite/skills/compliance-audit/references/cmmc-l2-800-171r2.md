# CMMC Level 2 — NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 Requirement Catalog

The full 110-requirement catalog backing the `comp-cmmc` module at Level 2.
**Load this file only when `comp-cmmc` is selected and L2 control mapping is
actually being performed** — the module's frontmatter and body are enough for
menu/recommendation purposes.

**Revision note:** CMMC 2.0 (32 CFR Part 170) assesses Level 2 against
**800-171 Revision 2** (110 requirements, 14 families). NIST has published Rev 3
(97 requirements, 17 families), but DoD adoption requires separate rulemaking —
map against Rev 2 until the contract or rule says otherwise, and note the Rev 3
transition as a watch item in the readiness call.

**Statement fidelity:** statements below are condensed to one line. They are
faithful to the official text but not verbatim — for SSP/POA&M language or
assessment evidence, cite the official publication (NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2,
csrc.nist.gov) rather than this file.

**Scoring:** SPRS self-assessment scoring uses the DoD Assessment Methodology
(110 max; deductions of 5/3/1 points per unmet requirement, weighted by
criticality — e.g. 3.5.3 MFA and 3.13.11 FIPS-validated crypto are 5-point
items). This file doesn't carry per-requirement weights; pull the current DoD
Assessment Methodology when a SPRS score is requested.

## Mapping guidance

- Assess each requirement Met / Partially Met / Not Met / Not Applicable **within
  the CUI boundary fixed in step 1 of the workflow** — never org-wide by default.
- Technical requirements (3.1, 3.3–3.5, 3.13, 3.14) are where security-audit
  findings land as evidence; 3.2, 3.9, 3.10, and parts of 3.6/3.7/3.12 need
  organizational/documentary evidence and cannot be inferred from a repo — cap
  the readiness claim accordingly and say so.
- 3.12.4 (SSP) and 3.12.2 (POA&M) are assessment-gating: a missing SSP fails an
  L2 assessment regardless of technical posture — check for these documents
  explicitly.
- **800-171A assessment objectives** (the per-requirement "determine if"
  decomposition used by C3PAOs, ~320 objectives) are NOT carried here — when
  objective-level evidence is requested, scope it as follow-up against the
  official 800-171A publication.
