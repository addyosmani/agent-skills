# CMMC 2.0 + NIST SP 800-171 / 800-172

## Coverage

**CMMC 2.0** — tiered maturity model for DoD contractors handling FCI/CUI:
- **L1** — 15 FAR safeguards, self-assessed annually (FCI).
- **L2** — 110 NIST 800-171 controls, self or third-party assessed every 3 years (CUI).
- **L3** — adds 800-172 enhanced controls, government-assessed.

Requirements **flow down to subcontractors**.

**NIST SP 800-171** protects CUI outside federal systems; **800-172** adds enhanced
controls for APT environments (aligns to CMMC L3).

## What to assess

- First fix the **level from the contract** — auditing L2 when the contract says
  L1 wastes effort; assuming L1 when CUI flows is a legal gap.
- Establish the **CUI/FCI boundary**: where the data lives, flows, and who touches
  it. Controls are assessed inside that boundary, not org-wide by default.
- **For L2, map requirement-by-requirement** against the full 110-requirement
  catalog in `../references/cmmc-l2-800-171r2.md` — load it when mapping begins,
  not before. It carries all 14 families, mapping guidance (CUI-boundary scoping,
  which families need documentary vs technical evidence, SSP/POA&M gating), and
  the Rev 2 vs Rev 3 watch note. Note SPRS self-assessment scoring posture where
  relevant.
- Statuses cite the requirement (`800-171:3.1.1`) with the CMMC level context.

## Cross-map anchors

- Derives from NIST baselines — the shared spine with **FedRAMP** means one
  800-53/800-171 mapping feeds both (`comp-nist-core`, `comp-fedramp`); split from
  FedRAMP as a module so "just FedRAMP" doesn't drag CMMC in, but the anchor still
  dedupes shared controls.
- Supply-chain flow-down pairs with **800-161** (`comp-cloud-supply-chain`).

## Coverage limits

L2 is carried at the **individual-requirement level** — all 110 Rev 2 requirements
enumerated in `../references/cmmc-l2-800-171r2.md` (statements condensed; cite the
official NIST text in SSP/POA&M language). 800-171A assessment-objective
decomposition (~320 objectives) and per-requirement SPRS point weights are NOT
carried — when objective-level evidence or a SPRS score is requested, scope it as
follow-up against the official 800-171A and DoD Assessment Methodology
publications rather than approximating from memory.
