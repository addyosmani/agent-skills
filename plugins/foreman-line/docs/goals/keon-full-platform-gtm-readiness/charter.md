## Objective

Bring Keon's full canonical platform portfolio and commercial services to a
truthful, evidence-backed GTM-ready state; make the External Application Core
and tailored program dossiers approval-ready; clear all agent-addressable
evidence, claim, product, service, website, pitch, legal-packet, and submission
preparation blockers; and preserve explicit human/counsel gates for filing,
legal conclusions, external submissions, publication, outreach, payment, and
production release.

This goal does not promise that every proposed capability becomes a shipped
product. It requires every capability to be correctly classified, evidenced,
marketed within its verified state, and either cleared for its intended surface
or explicitly held with an owner and stop condition.

## Acceptance requirements by critical artifact

### Master capability map

- Covers every canonical package, service, public surface, and relevant repo.
- Classifies maturity as implemented, demoable, production-ready,
  design-target, research, aspirational, deferred, or absent/contradictory.
- Names owner, evidence, verification date, disclosure class, permitted wording,
  prohibited detail, dependencies, and program relevance.
- Receives independent adversarial review.

### Evidence Ledger v1.2

- Contains no unresolved `[FILL]` field for an in-scope row.
- Every row has exact source revision, environment, positive and negative
  verification, limitations, expiry, maturity, and external-claim disposition.
- Unknown or unavailable evidence yields `No`, never an optimistic status.
- Reconciles KEO-54/202 and current Runtime/verifier evidence without treating
  draft Ledgerline terminology as public canon.
- Technical owners and an independent reviewer sign off on the populated set.

### External Application Core v1.2

- Every factual capability claim traces to a current cleared ledger row.
- Market and traction statements trace to real evidence or explicitly say none.
- Contains no frozen mechanism detail or retired-pack content.
- Has founder approval and recorded counsel clearance for its intended reusable
  purpose.
- Is not directly submitted wholesale; tailored dossiers preserve provenance.

### Full GTM readiness

- Each canonical package/service has a truthful page or approved no-page
  disposition, ICP/use case, offer/status, conversion path, fulfillment and
  support boundary, claims map, demo/rehearsal evidence, and release decision.
- Cross-product dependencies are explicit: Runtime may stand alone; Cortex
  requires Runtime governance; Collective requires Runtime and Cortex; MCP
  Gateway is a Runtime component; services are not product planes.
- Every public route and flagship-media claim is checked against the current
  claims/proof/evidence set and the intended live environment.
- Security, privacy, legal, billing, and production gates pass where the
  affected surface requires them.

## Exit criterion

This goal closes only when:

1. W0-W8 have accepted evidence or an explicit, owner-approved defer/hold that
   removes the affected availability or launch claim;
2. the Evidence Ledger has no unfilled in-scope rows;
3. the External Application Core and each selected program dossier are
   founder-approved, counsel-cleared for the intended surface, current, and
   submission-ready;
4. the Keon portfolio and all canonical services have traceable GTM and
   readiness dispositions without contradictory public claims;
5. the existing KPP-001-A revenue goal remains reconciled and its state is
   truthfully reflected, not falsely declared complete;
6. all chosen human milestones are recorded as completed, explicitly deferred,
   or blocked with a named external owner and exact next action; and
7. every merge, release, live-route, filing, submission, outreach, payment, or
   publication claimed complete has been verified in the real target system.

“Agent work complete, awaiting H2/H3” is an allowed precise stop state. It is
not equivalent to submitted, filed, launched, sold, or complete GTM.

## Stop conditions

Stop and return to the decision owner when:

- an existing coordinator owns the same parcel boundary and no transfer is
  recorded;
- a proposed package, product, service, or claim conflicts with canon;
- a capability cannot be classified from reproducible current evidence;
- application or public wording outruns the cleared Evidence Ledger;
- counsel must make a legal, ownership, filing, confidentiality, or rights
  determination;
- a task would expose protected source, invention detail, legal material, raw
  evidence, credentials, or customer data to Genspark or another external model;
- work would touch a dirty shared checkout instead of an isolated worktree;
- a parcel crosses its exact file/repo scope or a required review remains red;
- live Linear, repository, website, CI, or program state contradicts the plan;
  or
- any outward-facing or irreversible action lacks explicit authority.
